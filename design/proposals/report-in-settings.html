Where the report lives

Proposal, 2026-10-19, following report.html. The report is a debugging dump: what someone helping you asks for when they need to see your computer ("go to Settings, Diagnostics, Save a report, and send me the file"). report.html put it in the log window's title row, beside Copy for helpdesk, where it's the first thing anyone opening the log sees, and the two read as two answers to "what do I send?". This moves it out of sight, to where you'd be told to find it.

A: Settings → Diagnostics

A section of its own, before About: easy to say over the phone, and a name that tells the person sent there they're in the right place. Nothing else lives there today; it's where later debugging aids would go.

Or in a terminal: crake report

The row says whether the places you connected to are in it, as Privacy & log's switch has it (off by default today, as drawn); switched on: "The places you connected to are left out (Privacy & log)." The terminal line is each system's own command: crake report on Linux, /Applications/Crake.app/Contents/MacOS/crake report on a Mac, "C:\Program Files\Crake\crake.exe" report on Windows (neither puts crake on the PATH).

Saving (a few seconds; longer when the internet is what's broken), then saved or not

The button keeps its words and its width while saving, only greyed out, so the row doesn't shift. What's happening has a box of its own under the row, on the surface tint: Crake's dashed head, its dashes going round (its sign for "under way": the tray's while a tunnel connects, New tunnel's while it asks a gateway; ink, not teal, which is for decisions; still when animations are off), and after 2 s how long it's been, so a slow save doesn't look stuck, then the result in the same box, so it appears once and changes in place.

Never passwords, sign-ins or keys. It names the places you connected to; Privacy & log can leave them out. It stays on this computer: open it and read it before you send it.

What's in it, and what never is

  • In it: the tunnels and their state; this computer's DNS settings, routes and Crake's firewall rules, as the system reports them (another VPN client's domains among them); whether one.one.one.one answers, and each connected tunnel's most recently used host that has a name, and which way; the last day of Crake's decisions; Crake's service log for the last hour; the computer's own network addresses and its router's, without which the routes say nothing.
  • Never in it, whatever the setting: passwords, sign-ins (session cookies, OpenVPN's tokens, sign-in pages), keys, certificates, profiles, file paths, web addresses, and hardware (MAC) addresses: a Mac's route list names the router's and other devices', Windows' each adapter's; they're taken out.
  • The hosts you reached (names and addresses, IPv4 and IPv6) follow "Hide names and addresses when sharing". It's off by default today, so a report names them unless it's switched on; the row says which, before you save.

Not shown

  • About, where a Mac keeps its System Report: About is about Crake; the report is about this computer, and "Diagnostics" is the word a helper reaches for.
  • A menu in the log window ("…" beside Copy for helpdesk): the log has none, and it would put the report back beside the copy, one click further away.

What else follows

  • The report's parts, the hiding and crake report stay. New: hardware (MAC) addresses are taken out (a Mac's netstat -rn, Windows' route print adapter list), and every line goes through the same filter, Crake's own decisions and tunnel lines too (a stopped tunnel's reason can carry what openconnect last said); a line naming sign-in material becomes "(a line left out: it named a sign-in)".
  • The "?" on "Hide names and addresses when sharing" says reports are in Diagnostics.
  • The screens: log.html loses the button, settings.html gains the section; DESIGN.md's screens table follows.
  • DESIGN.md's "Directions we tried and rejected" gains one: "Save a report…" beside "Copy for helpdesk" in the log's title row (report.html, A), two answers to "what do I send?".
  • In the app: a Diagnostics section with the save and its state (moved from the log window), CRAKE_SHOW=settings:diagnostics, the window tests moved with it, and the journeys saving their report from Settings.
  • The CHANGELOG's report line (still unreleased) says Settings → Diagnostics; CLAUDE.md likewise.

Recommendation: A

The log goes back to one thing to paste, the report is out of the way of anyone who doesn't need it, and someone helping you can say where it is in one breath: "go to Settings, Diagnostics, Save a report". B saves a section but puts the dump among privacy settings.
