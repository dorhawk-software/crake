After: the row stays a row; what the bird says lives in its "?"
Title per system, a small badge after it in the bar's own colour, and the "?" card (shown open) with all six states as sentences. The row is as tall as its neighbours and its text starts where theirs does.

  • "Tray" on Linux and Windows (Windows 11's own Settings: Taskbar → Other system tray icons); "menu bar" on macOS. Not "panel": that is Crake's own popover ("Undo stays in the panel either way").
