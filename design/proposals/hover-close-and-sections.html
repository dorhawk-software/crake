Hover on the round × and on Settings' sections (asked for). Pointing at the × darkens its circle a step (the line colour; in dark the strong line, as the line alone barely shows there); pointing at a section that isn't the current one gives it a soft background, the line colour, so the current one (the panel's white or dark) still stands out. Every window's × alike: Log, Settings, tunnel detail, Traffic and New tunnel.
