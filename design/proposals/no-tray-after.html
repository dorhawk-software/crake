When nothing shows tray icons (after the impeccable critique of no-tray.html, 24/40). Stock GNOME on Fedora (and Debian) shows no tray icons without its AppIndicator extension. Today Crake then starts with nothing on screen at all, and Settings offers a switch that does nothing. Crake hears when a tray appears or goes (the StatusNotifierWatcher on the session bus), and asks GNOME about the extension (org.gnome.Shell.Extensions).
The row in Settings → General, by what Crake finds

Fedora installs it with Crake (a Recommends) and leaves it off. The button asks GNOME Shell to switch it on (EnableExtension); the bird appears at once and the row is plain again. The switch stays the user’s preference, operable, in every state.

E. Started by hand with no tray: the panel, with a note
Opened from the app grid (which hands Crake focus), the panel opens centred near the top of the screen, not at a tray corner that has no icon, with this note on top. Neutral, not amber: the tray is optional and Crake works fully without it. No teal: a bordered action, a muted “Not now”, as the shipped notices. Installed or missing: B’s or C’s words and remedy.

F. Started at login with no tray: one notification
At login GNOME opens the Overview and doesn’t give autostarted windows focus, so no window. Crake waits ~10 s for a tray to appear (a slow login), then sends one notification, once for each case it finds; it stays in GNOME’s message list. Switching “Show the bird in the tray” off ends every note and notification.

  • With “Show the bird in the tray” off, none of this shows: the row is plain and nothing is said at start.
  • Crake decides by whether a tray is there (the StatusNotifierWatcher on the session bus), not by the desktop’s name; GNOME’s extension (both its IDs, Ubuntu’s and upstream’s) only picks the words.
  • Linux only: Windows and macOS always have a notification area / menu bar.
  • "Switch on AppIndicator" asks GNOME Shell over D-Bus (EnableExtension); no password, no terminal.
