A host's whole name. In tunnel detail a long name is cut with "…" (it has to fit). Pointing at it shows the whole name, and it can be copied whole, not just what's visible. The same for the rows under "From the gateway".
A. Point to read it, click to copy it (recommended)
Every name in the list works the same way, long or short. At rest none is underlined. The one under the pointer gets a dotted underline and a hand cursor (shown here with the pointer on the long name), and after a short pause a small card under it shows the whole name and "Click to copy". A click copies the whole name, and the card says "Copied".

B. A copy icon on the row
The card shows the whole name only; a copy icon appears after the name while the row is pointed at, and copies it. One more thing on the row, and it lands on top of the name when the name is cut.
