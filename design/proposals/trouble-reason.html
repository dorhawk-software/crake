Why a tunnel stopped. When a tunnel drops, the panel says "Couldn't stay connected", whatever happened. craked often knows more: an OpenVPN 3 session that expired, a gateway that ended the session, a sign-in refused. The log already has the whole sentence; the panel and the tunnel's detail don't. When the reason is one Crake recognises (table below), say it; otherwise keep "Couldn't stay connected". The detail window shows the whole sentence in both options.
A. The status names the reason (recommended)
The red words after the name say what happened, in two or three words. Nothing else in the panel changes; the switch is the action. The whole sentence is in the tunnel's detail and the log.
