When nothing shows tray icons. Stock GNOME on Fedora (and Debian) shows no tray icons without its AppIndicator extension. Today Crake then starts with nothing on screen at all, and Settings offers a switch that does nothing. Crake hears when a tray appears or goes (the StatusNotifierWatcher on the session bus), and asks GNOME about the extension (org.gnome.Shell.Extensions).
