How long it waits.
  • Until you choose: the notice and both triangles go, and the name works at once.
  • Not now: the notice and the triangles go. The log row keeps Use for the rest of the day. Crake doesn’t ask about that name again for a day, as now.
  • An hour unanswered: the notice and the triangles go. The person who typed the name is waiting now or not at all, and a browser looks up a word typed as a search too, so a stray search won’t keep the bird amber all day. The log row keeps Use until the day is over.
  • A tunnel it names goes off: its choice goes. When none is left, the notice and the triangles go.
  • Kept across restarts: the app reads the question from the log (kept on disk), so restarting Crake or craked doesn’t lose it or bring it back after you answered.
  • Several at once: one notice each, newest first, above the other notices. Rare: a name is asked once a day.
