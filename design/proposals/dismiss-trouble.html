Dismissing a stopped tunnel. A tunnel that stops on its own (session expired, the gateway ended it) stays red in the panel, with the tray's red badge, until it's switched on again. Switching on and off just to clear it is tedious. Dismissing puts it back to plain "Off": the red words and the tray's badge go, and the reason stays in the log and in the tunnel's detail. Nothing else changes: the tunnel isn't switched on or signed out.

The tray: the badge on the head as it is

Today the trouble icon is always the head awake with the badge, so a tunnel that stopped overnight with nothing else up looks as if something were still connected. Proposed: the badge goes on whatever the head would show without it: asleep when nothing is up, awake when something is.
Today, nothing up: awake, badge
Proposed, nothing up: asleep, badge
Proposed, another tunnel up: awake, badge (as today)
Dismissed, nothing up: asleep

How it works

  • craked keeps it: dismissing turns the tunnel from trouble to off in craked (a new D-Bus call, guarded like switching), so it stays dismissed after the app restarts, and the log says nothing new.
  • The reason stays findable: the tunnel's detail keeps the sentence under its title until the tunnel next connects ("Stopped at 03:01. OpenVPN 3 ended its session: tun0 is gone."), and the log has it.
  • Today's other cause is fixed separately: Globex wasn't really down this morning. openvpn3 reconnected it at 03:01 after the Wi-Fi dropped, and Crake called it stopped in the second its device was being made again (fixed: Crake now says "Globex is reconnecting." and follows the new device).
