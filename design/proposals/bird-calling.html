The crake, calling

The head is raised in the corncrake’s call, the bill short, stout and parted at the tip (a mouth, even at bar size), the eye a square hole on whole pixels, and the folded wing one straight cut across the breast. The richness is the aperture’s kind, cuts rather than shading, and each cut is a feature: the mouth, the eye, the wing. The gesture does the rest: beside the faceted aperture, the cube and the badge, this is the one icon in the bar doing something. In the bar it stays one ink, as DESIGN.md asks, with the teal marks, red badge and amber triangle where they are today; the corncrake’s colours go on the app icon. You asked to see both, so the same drawing in colour is beside it, with what it costs. Every icon below is drawn at the pixel size the tray shows and enlarged without smoothing: what you judge is what the bar shows.

Every state, enlarged

Asleep: the head drops 25° below level, the eye closes, the ink dims to 45 %. Connecting: the outline dashed, the bill shut. Listening: the bird. Routed: the bill opens with the two teal marks in front of it. Needs you and trouble: the amber triangle or red badge behind the head, a gap cut round it, clear of the eye and the bill. On a light bar the ink turns dark and the marks take the light theme’s teal, amber and red, as today.

B, the coloured bird in the bar: what it costs

  • The bill goes. Flesh-pink sits at the breast’s value on a dark bar and on a light one; the one feature that says “bird” becomes a smudge, and at 16 px the bird is a brown-capped loaf.
  • DESIGN.md’s rule that the head changes shape, never colour, and that colour in the bar means a decision or a state, would need a new line: the bird’s own colours mean nothing.
  • On a light bar the pale breast has to turn slate (the tray turns greys dark today; it would need a second rule for these three colours), so it is a different bird on light and dark bars.
  • The amber triangle sits on the chestnut crown; with its gap it still reads, but the two are neighbours on the colour wheel. And beside your neighbours the chestnut is the dullest colour in the bar, where the one-ink bird is the brightest thing there, like the aperture.

Ruled out this round

  • Long straight cuts along straight edges (a cap band, a back band, the bill cut off by a vertical gap): a door frame with a play button. Cuts have to cross curves.
  • The bill level, head not raised: a penguin in one ink.
  • Two bars across the wing for the corncrake’s barred flank: stripes on a sail at real size.
  • The head raised at 16 px: the grid can’t hold the angle; 16 px keeps the bill level and the rest of the drawing.
  • Tipping the whole bird to sleep (as today’s head tips): the base swung out of the slot as a thorn; the head dropping on its neck keeps the base where it is and gives the motion somewhere to go.
The choice: A, the calling crake in one ink in the bar, the app icon in colour, or B, the same bird in the corncrake’s colours in the bar too. Recommended: A. In your bar it is the brightest, simplest mark beside the gradient cube because it is the one white figure with a gesture; colour costs nothing on the app icon, where the bird is unmistakably a crake, and costs the bill in the bar.
