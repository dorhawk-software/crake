Critique fixes: before and after

From the design critique of 15 October 2026 (impeccable, 28/40). Each issue: the app as it is today, captured in demo mode, next to a mockup of the fix. The mockups are drawn from the design files the app was built from, so small sample differences (numbers, a column) aren't part of a proposal; the change described above each one is. Nothing in the app changes until you choose.

1 · P1The profile editor loses edits on Escape, × or Cancel

With unsaved changes, Escape, × and Cancel no longer close the window: the footer asks in place, with Keep editing as the default (Escape or Enter again keeps editing). With nothing changed, Escape closes as today.

Live mockup: editor-after.html

2 · P2Traffic draws traffic volume in teal

DESIGN.md: teal means only “Crake made a decision”; its charts show volume in greys and the moment Crake routed as a teal tick. After: the strip's bars in greys, a teal tick in each 5-second slot where Crake routed a new host, tunnel boxes outlined grey like Direct. The teal packets on tunnel paths stay. The middle picture is the design Traffic was built from, so the only difference from the “after” is the colour.

Live mockup: traffic-after.html

3 · P2New tunnel shows nine protocols after the gateway already answered

Once detection has answered, the list folds into one quiet row, “Choose the protocol yourself · 8 more”, and Group comes up into view; a hairline marks the footer. Opened, the protocols sit under two subheads (used with real gateways / Beta) instead of six Beta badges. While detecting, or when detection fails, the full list shows as today.

Live mockup: newtunnel-after.html

4 · P2The leftover notice: Copy, Got it, and the header's bird

Copy becomes a button inside the command (no teal in an amber box), Got it a real button, one line says the note goes by itself, and the header's bird wears the same amber triangle as the bird in the bar.

Live mockup: notice-after.html

5 · P3Tunnel detail's Learned list is a grid of buttons

Pin, Unpin and Forget lose their borders and stay visible; pointed at, they get a fill and Forget turns red. The Learned tab counts learned hosts only (4; the pinned one is under Pinned 1).

Live mockup: detail-after.html
