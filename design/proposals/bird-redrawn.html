The calling crake

After three rounds and four independent critiques. Your bird has as much ink as its neighbours (measured from your screenshot); it reads plain because it is one outline with one hole, a dome on a column that reads as a ghost or a chess pawn, its long edges soft between pixels. The aperture beside it is just as white and one colour; it looks richer because of its straight cuts. So this bird changes its outline and gains cuts, and stays one ink in the bar, as DESIGN.md asks: a crake’s bust, the breast pushed forward and running off the edge, a short conical bill, a small eye; where there is room (32 px, what your 2× bar actually shows) the bill opens in the crake’s call (crex crex), its two halves spreading from the hinge. 16 px is placed pixel by pixel; 22 and 32 px have their straight edges on whole pixels. The corncrake’s own colours go to the app icon, where they cost nothing in the tray’s states.

In your bar

Your bar is at 2×: icons are about 30 device pixels, so the 32 px drawing is the one you see. At 1× (most other screens) the tray takes the 16 or 22 px drawing.

Every state

Off, connecting, listening, routed (teal call marks), needs you (amber), trouble (red). Badges sit on the back of the head, as DESIGN.md says, clear of the bill and the eye; at 16 px they are a coloured dot or triangle (no room for the “!”). Light bars use the light theme’s teal, amber and red.

The family: panel header and app icon

The panel header uses the 32 px drawing at 24 px, with the call marks. The app icon is the same bird in the corncrake’s colours (a buff-chestnut cap with barred nape, a blue-grey face and breast, a flesh-pink bill), a few reeds of its meadow, and the call in teal; a one-ink version beside it, for comparison.

What the critiques ruled out

  • Detail on today’s shape (a shaded throat, cut lines): more drawing, still a ghost.
  • The two-tone bird (the breast in half-white): its grey is off’s grey, so half of the awake bird looks asleep.
  • Colour in the tray: the chestnut is the dullest thing in a dark bar, the amber and red badges merge into it, and the light bar washes it out. It would also break DESIGN.md’s rule that the bird changes shape, never colour.
  • An app tile in the tray: foreign to GNOME and Windows trays, and the bird inside shrinks.
  • An open bill at 16 px: reads as a fork; it opens from 22 px.
  • Notches for the barred nape in the tray: read as glitches at real size; the bars stay, painted, in the app icon.
