5 · Your OpenVPN 3 tunnels move

Once, when this Crake first starts: each OpenVPN 3 tunnel's profile is copied into Crake's own OpenVPN, and it says so in the log. A tunnel that's on moves when it's next switched on. A profile OpenVPN 3 keeps locked can't be read: that tunnel stays on OpenVPN 3 and its notice asks for the .ovpn file (the amber notice of leftover-warning.html, 3: it needs you, nothing has broken yet).
