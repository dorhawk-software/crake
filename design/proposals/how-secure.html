Because a VPN shouldn’t be a chore.
Source on GitHub·dorhawk.software
Is it safe?
Does anything leave this computer?
No. Crake decides everything here: there’s no Crake server, no account and no telemetry. Only your VPN’s own sign-in talks to your company.
Can Crake break my internet?
If Crake stops, its routes and rules go with it, and your traffic goes straight out as it would without Crake. A tunnel that drops takes only what went through it.
What does my company see?
Only what goes through its tunnel: the hosts that need it. Everything else goes straight out, not through the company.
Who can change what?
Changes need you, at this computer. A tunnel your IT set up can be switched on and off, but its rules can’t be widened.
Crake is free and stays free: no ads, no tracking, nothing locked. If it saved you a headache, you can buy its developer a coffee. It’s a thank-you, not a subscription.
