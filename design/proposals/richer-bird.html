A more drawn bird. Next to faceted and coloured icons the head reads plain: one solid shape with a hole. DESIGN.md keeps the bar icon symbolic (one ink, the head changes shape, never colour; colour only for the state marks), so every direction here adds drawing, not colour: cut lines the bar shows through, a second tone of the same white, the corncrake’s own markings. Each is shown in a dark bar beside stand-ins for the neighbours in your screenshot, at the sizes trays really use (16, 20, 22 px), enlarged pixel by pixel so what you see is what the bar shows.
