When the "Saved" line goes

Proposal, 2026-10-19, following report-in-settings.html (built). After saving, Settings → Diagnostics says "Saved crake-report-….txt in Documents." with Open and Show in folder. Today the line stays until Settings closes, through other sections and back; the user: "not nice from a UX point of view". The line answers "did it work, and where is it?" right after the save. Once you've moved on, it's yesterday's news, and coming back to it reads as if something were still going on.

A: It goes when you move on (recommended)

The line is about the save you just made, so it lasts as long as you stay with it: choosing another section, or closing Settings, takes it away, and coming back shows the row as it was before saving. Staying with it means everything else: Open (reading the file first, as the row asks) and Show in folder, switching to the mail or chat app to attach it, the window losing focus, a click elsewhere in Diagnostics. A second "Save a report…" keeps the old line while its dialog is open (cancelled, nothing changed), and "Saving the report…" takes its place once a file is chosen. The line also says whether names were left out, as the file it names has them: today's file and the one saved after switching hiding on share a name.

One rule covers a save still under way: leaving takes away a result, never a save in progress. Leave while it saves, and back in Diagnostics it's still saving, or its result is there; leaving after that takes the result away as above. A failure follows the same rule as a success, not a timer: its reason may decide what you do next (another folder). Close Settings while it saves and the file is still written; a quiet notification then says how it went ("Saved crake-report-2026-10-19.txt in Documents."), as the Notifications setting allows.

B: It fades after a while

Gone ten seconds after saving, wherever you are. Not chosen: Open and Show in folder would vanish under the pointer of someone who reads slowly or was looking for the file; a line that disappears on a timer says nothing to someone who looked away; and reduce motion leaves it to jump out instead.

Also not chosen

Keeping the line through Privacy & log, the natural detour: back from switching hiding on, "Saved …" would describe a file that still names your hosts. Taking it away once the file is opened: Show in folder comes after reading.

C: A ✕ on the line

It stays until you close it. Not chosen: a chore that adds nothing (nobody needs to keep it), and a second small control beside Open and Show in folder.

Today: it stays until Settings closes

What the user saw: back in Diagnostics after looking elsewhere, the line is still there, as if the save had just happened.

Recommendation: A

The line belongs to the moment of saving and to the place you saved from. It goes when you leave that place, never under your pointer, and a save you didn't wait for still gets to tell you how it went.
