A tray badge you can read

Proposal, 2026-10-18; B chosen by the user the same day and built. The user, with a screenshot of GNOME's bar: the trouble and warning badges are "extremely teeny tiny and pretty much impossible to read"; another app's count badge beside it read at once.

Two things make today's badge unreadable at the bar's 16 px. It is small: a circle 9/32 of the icon, 4.5 px across. And it sits between pixels: its centre and its "!" land on half pixels, so the "!" is a smudge of half-inked pixels, while the bird's eye is snapped to whole pixels at every size. Every option below is drawn by Crake's own code (tray/art.rs, the drawing the bar gets), the badge on whole pixels in A, B and C, and the enlarged tiles are those 16 px images, pixel for pixel.

The neighbour in each bar is a stand-in for another app's icon with a count badge, for scale. On a light bar (Windows' light taskbar, macOS' light menu bar) Crake draws the bird dark; today its badge keeps the dark-bar colours there.

Recommendation: B

Bottom right, a 7 px circle at 16 px with a "!" one pixel wide, centred on its pixels: it reads at 16 px, it leaves the head, the eye and the bill whole (asleep at 16 px, the bill's underside thins by a row), and the bird keeps its breast. C reads a little more at 16 px but takes most of the breast and the tip of the sleeping bill. A shows that snapping alone isn't enough: today's place has no room for a "!" at 16 px.

What comes with it

  • On a light bar, the light theme's colours. Red #B42318 with a white "!", amber #BF7E12 with a dark "!" (DESIGN.md's danger and warning). Today's dark-bar red is 2.4:1 against a light taskbar and its amber 1.5:1, under the 3:1 a shape needs.
  • Every size on whole pixels, as the eye already is: the circle an odd or even number of pixels across, centred to match, the "!" a stem, a gap and a dot that keep the circle's middle, smaller in a small circle and left out where there's no room.
  • The same rules otherwise. Red means a tunnel stopped, amber that Crake needs you; red wins. The badge sits on the bird as it would be without it (asleep, connecting or awake), with a gap cut so it reads on any bar, and grows in about its own centre. A routed bird with a badge shows the badge instead of the call marks, as today.
  • DESIGN.md's tray table says "behind the head"; with B it says "at the bottom right, over the breast". Already ruled out, and not tried again here: colouring the bird itself (the bird changes shape, never colour) and room above the head (it shrinks the bird).
