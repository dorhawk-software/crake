Kept as is

  • The reason under every decision, with one number: Flighty's identity, Little Snitch's corresponding rule.
  • One mark, one meaning. The two-stroke tick is Things' checkbox: the mark of the act is the identity. Teal stays rare because it marks only decisions.
  • The switch is the state; the row's second line says it in words. GNOME's VPN rows have only the switch.
  • The panel's shape: a Windows flyout's (summary, tasks, one link) without tiles; notices in the GNOME HIG's banner shape.
  • The fallback menu, one level deep, like Tailscale's Linux tray and WireGuard's menu.
  • The sign-in window: one state per view, each with its next step, including "Open the page again".
  • New tunnel says the consequence where the choice is made ("Full tunnel. Crake won't learn anything.").
  • Sentence case and periods, against GNOME's header capitals.
  • Motion, then rest; one sound for one moment.

Dropped

  • A map of connections (Little Snitch, Mullvad, Proton): "The internet is never drawn."
  • State told by colour, and marks that need a legend (Mullvad, Little Snitch, Tailscale's dot grid): the bird changes shape, not colour; buttons name the action.
  • Blinking, flashing, shimmer or a pulsing "trying again": the rejected breathing dot in another form.
  • Counters and charts as reassurance (Proton's NetShield counts, Flighty's lifetime stats): numbers that answer no question.
  • A modal alert that holds the connection (Little Snitch, LuLu), a three-pane monitor, per-app routing.
  • "Expires in" on a hand-added host and "Today / Earlier" headings in Learned: a second decision in a row that has one; a heading that splits nothing.
  • Teenage Engineering's hardware look, Flighty's split-flap board, Panic's jokes, Things' pop on every act, Linear's themes, confetti for the first route: all on DESIGN.md's rejected list.
  • "Last heard from the gateway 6 s ago" (WireGuard) and other names on a routed address, per row (Cloudflare): claims the app can't make today; written down for later.

Platform findings (GNOME hides Low-urgency banners, the AppIndicator click, Windows toasts' sender and the tray overflow, Win11's 8 px corners, the Mac's notch) are in crake-vs-references.md, for docs/engineering.
