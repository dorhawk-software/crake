Approving Crake's service on a Mac. With craked registered by the app itself (SMAppService), System Settings lists it as Crake, not under the signing certificate's name. macOS then asks the user to switch it on once, in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions; until then nothing is routed. Crake has to say so, and take the user there. The same words show if the user switches the service off later.
